Festival Easter

Easter, observed on the first Sunday after the first full moon following the vernal equinox, is a Christian holiday commemorating the resurrection of Jesus Christ. This global event, prominent in Christian-majority countries such as the United States, Germany, and the Philippines, features church services, Easter parades, egg hunts, and family gatherings. The Easter Bunny, a symbol of new life, adds to the festive atmosphere.

Festival Ramadan

Ramadan, a month-long Islamic festival, involves fasting from dawn until sunset, increased prayer, and reflection on the Quran. Celebrated in Muslim-majority countries like Egypt, Indonesia, and Saudi Arabia, this observance follows the Islamic lunar calendar, moving 10-12 days earlier each year. Iftar meals at sunset break the fast, emphasizing charity and community service.

Flora Highlight Cherry Blossom Season

Cherry blossoms, blooming from March to April, are a seasonal highlight symbolizing renewal and the fleeting nature of life. These delicate pink and white flowers, particularly celebrated in Japan during 'Hanami', represent peace and transient beauty. Their picturesque canopy creates a beloved spring spectacle.
